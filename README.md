# 📊 Global Superstore: Business Intelligence Analysis

AnalystLab Africa | Data Analytics Internship Week 2

Power BI dashboard analyzing global retail sales, profitability, and regional performance, built for a Junior BI Analyst case study.

🎯 Objective

Analyze the Global Superstore Dataset (51,290 orders, 2011–2014) to help management monitor sales performance, identify top-performing regions/segments/categories, and deliver data-driven recommendations.

🛠️ Tools

Power BI (Power Query, DAX, dashboard design) • Data cleaning & transformation • KPI & visualization design

📈 Snapshot
Sales	Profit	Orders	Avg. Sale	Margin
$12.64M	$1.47M	25,035	$246.49	11.6%
📝 Executive Summary

Insights: Sales grew 90% (2011–2014) but margin sits at 11.6% overall. Central/South lead in sales; Canada/North Asia lead in margin. Technology is the strongest category (14.0% margin); Furniture the weakest (6.9%). Consumer drives 51.5% of revenue.

Risks: Tables sub-category operates at a net loss (-8.5% margin, 29% avg. discount). 60% of sales depend on Standard Class shipping. Southeast Asia & EMEA run near break-even.

Opportunities: Scale Technology & Office Supplies further. Expand in high-margin markets (Canada, North Asia, Central Asia). Replicate top-product profit patterns.

Recommendations: Cap discounting on loss-making sub-categories → reallocate investment to high-margin categories → audit underperforming regions → pilot expansion in high-margin markets → diversify shipping dependency.
